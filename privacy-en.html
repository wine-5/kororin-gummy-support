Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 (updated November 4, 2026)

This policy explains how YutoImata ("we", "us") handles information in the iOS app Kororin Gummy ("the app").

The app has no accounts. It does not ask for or read your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise (GPS) location.

1. Information stored only on your device

The app keeps the following on your device. It is never sent to our servers, and we cannot see it.

  • Your progress (cleared stages, stars collected, best results)
  • Your settings (music and sound volume, language, and similar options)
  • Whether you have purchased "Remove Ads"
  • Your crystal balance in the shop, the skins, trails and line colors you bought, and the record of crystal-pack purchases (also kept in your device's secure storage, the Keychain; they do not carry over to a new device)

You can play the app without an internet connection. The app only goes online to show ads and to talk to the App Store (to show the prices of "Remove Ads" and the crystal packs, to buy them and to restore purchases).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve these ads, AdMob may automatically collect and use information such as:

  • Your IP address and the approximate region derived from it
  • Device information such as model, OS version, and language setting
  • How many times ads were shown and whether you tapped them
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking as described below
  • Diagnostic information the ad software (Google Mobile Ads SDK) uses to check how it is working, such as crash logs, performance information like launch time, and other diagnostic data

This information goes to Google and is handled under Google's policies. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. To learn how Google uses this information, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies

Permission to track

The app uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency to ask whether the advertising identifier may be used. If you allow it, you may see ads based on your interests. If you don't, you can still play everything in the app; the identifier is not used and the ads you see are not based on your interests.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone. You can manage Google ad settings at My Ad Center.

Where required by law, the app may ask for your consent to this use of information before showing ads.

3. In-app purchases

"Remove Ads" and the shop's crystal packs (consumable items that add crystals) are purchased through Apple's App Store. Crystal packs are not covered by "Restore Purchases", but the crystals you bought are also kept in your device's secure storage (the Keychain), so they come back when you reinstall the app on the same device. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your credit card or other payment details. The app only receives the price of the item and whether the purchase succeeded, and stores the purchase result on your device. If Apple refunds a purchase, the app removes it from that record when Apple tells it so.

4. What the app does not use

The app contains no analytics or crash reporting tools that send information to us. (As described in section 2, the ad software may send its own diagnostic information to Google.) It has no features that connect you with other players.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with any third party other than the advertising service described in Section 2, except when disclosure is required by law.

6. When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and the contents of your message only to reply and follow up. We delete them once they are no longer needed.

7. Children

Anyone can play the app, but it is not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child uses the app, we ask a parent or guardian to review the tracking permission with them.

8. Deleting your data

  • The app has no button to delete data. To erase your progress (cleared stages, stars and settings), delete the app from your device. Deleting it removes the progress stored on the device.
  • Your crystals and purchase record are also kept in your device's secure storage (the Keychain), so they stay after you delete the app. If you reinstall it on the same device, your crystals and purchased items come back. "Remove Ads" can also be restored with "Restore Purchases". They may not come back if you erase the device or move to a new one.
  • To stop the advertising identifier from being used, turn off tracking for the app in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone. For information held by Google, see Google's pages linked above.

We do not keep any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you would like us to delete an email you sent us, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or the law change. We will announce important changes on this page and in the app's update notes. The updated policy takes effect when it is posted here.
